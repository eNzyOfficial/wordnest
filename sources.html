Sources and licences

Wordnest is built only from open data. The dictionary data is a combination of the sources below, filtered, merged and shortened for offline use, and is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Wordnest is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of these sources or with any other dictionary app.

Wiktionary

English definitions, pronunciations, word forms and synonyms; Thai translations; Thai entries with romanisation, IPA and classifiers. Content by Wiktionary contributors, extracted with Wiktextract by Tatu Ylonen (kaikki.org).
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

Volubilis Multilingual Thai Dictionary

Thai-English entries and the reverse English-Thai index. VOLUBILIS Multilingual Thai Dict. and Database by Belisan (Francis Bastien), v26.2.
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

Open English WordNet

English definitions, examples and synonyms. Based on Princeton WordNet; notice below.
Licence: CC BY 4.0

Thai WordNet (NICT)

Extra Thai equivalents for English senses, via the Open Multilingual Wordnet. Notice below.
Licence: Thai WordNet licence

CMUdict

US pronunciations where Wiktionary has none (converted from ARPAbet to IPA). Notice below.
Licence: BSD-style

Tatoeba

Example sentences in English and English-Thai pairs, by Tatoeba contributors.
Licence: CC BY 2.0 FR

ALT Parallel Corpus (NICT)

English-Thai example sentences from the Asian Language Treebank parallel corpus, via OPUS.
Licence: CC BY 4.0

FrequencyWords

Word frequency lists by Hermit Dave, used to rank suggestions, show frequency stars and pick the word of the day.
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

Wikimedia Commons

Recorded pronunciations are played from Wikimedia Commons when you are online and are not stored in the app. Each file page on Commons lists its author and licence. Offline, your device voice reads the word instead.
Licence: Per file
